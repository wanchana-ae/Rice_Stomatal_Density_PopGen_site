Same 24 genes, but each gene's SNPs are merged (union plink --bmerge) with the matching region of the 3K RGP quality-filtered panel, plus a "Combined" tree pooling all genes. A dropdown switches between the Combined tree and 21 individual genes (3 of 24 had too few SNPs even after merging).

How it was built

From SNP panel to interactive trees.

  1. Combined treeIQ-TREE (GTR+ASC, 1000 UFBoot / 1000 SH-aLRT) on SNPs pooled across all 24 candidate genes, 553 accessions.
  2. Per-gene treesrun_all_gene_trees.sh built an independent IQ-TREE per gene; 20 of 24 genes had enough SNPs to resolve a tree (4 skipped — too few SNPs).
  3. Relabeling & QCTips matched against variety_metadata.csv and population_groups_K2/K3/K5.csv; unmatched tips shown as UNKNOWN_<code>.
  4. Interactive viewerD3-based tree viewer with export (PNG/JPG/PDF/CSV) built on top of the verified tip mapping.
